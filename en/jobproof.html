The app for plumbers, electricians, heating engineers and other tradespeople: before/after photos, client signature, a professional PDF in your name, and one-tap sending via WhatsApp.

What JobProof does

Report in 5 steps

Client, work done, photos, price, signature. A draft is saved automatically.

Before / after photos

Take photos in the app or pick them from the gallery. They appear in the PDF.

Client signature

The client signs with a finger on your phone: proof of your visit and of acceptance of the work.

Professional PDF

Your name, your logo, your VAT number, the price breakdown, the photos and the signature.

Price and Belgian VAT

Hourly rate, lines for materials, travel and flat fees. VAT at 0%, 6%, 12% or 21%. Totals excl. VAT, VAT and incl. VAT calculated for you.

Send via WhatsApp

Open the client's chat with a ready-made message, then share the PDF via WhatsApp, e-mail or Drive.

Works offline

No internet needed on site: everything is stored on your phone.

Clients and history

Client records, job history, search, and reminders for drafts or reports not yet sent.

Which trades?

Plumbing, electrical, heating and air conditioning, painting, carpentry, tiling, roofing, gardening, cleaning, IT… Each job gets the icon of its trade. Any repair or installation work for a household or a business fits.

Install the app (Android)

  1. Download the APK with the button above, from your phone's browser (Chrome).
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks for permission to “Install unknown apps”, allow it for Chrome.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose “Install anyway”: the app is not yet published on the Play Store.
  4. Open JobProof, then enter your business details, logo and hourly rate in the Profile tab.
Beta version. JobProof is being tested with tradespeople in Belgium. You may run into bugs. Your feedback helps us improve it: tell us what is missing, what gets in the way and what you would like.

Your data and your privacy

  • No account, no sign-up. The app sends nothing to our servers.
  • Clients, reports, photos and signatures stay only on your phone.
  • The camera is only used when you tap “Take a photo”. Notifications (reminders) are local and can be turned off in the Profile.
  • Remember to keep your important PDFs: if you uninstall the app, local data is deleted.
  • See our privacy policy (in French).

Good to know

  • JobProof produces a job report, not a legal Belgian invoice. To invoice, use your usual invoicing tool with the amounts from the report.
  • For now: Android only. An iPhone version may follow depending on feedback.
  • Free during the test period.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes, during the test period. If a paid plan is offered later, you will be told in advance.

Why install it outside the Play Store?

This is a test version distributed directly. It is signed, and the SHA-256 fingerprint above lets you verify the file. A Play Store release is planned for later.

Can I use my own logo?

Yes: Profile tab → “Logo”. Without a logo, the PDF shows the icon of your trade instead.

Are photos and signatures included in the PDF?

Yes, along with the price breakdown, VAT and the total incl. VAT.

How do I send the report to the client?

On the job screen, “Message the client on WhatsApp” opens the chat with a ready-made message, then “Share PDF” lets you send it via WhatsApp, e-mail or another app.

What about iPhone?

Not yet. Let us know if you need it: it influences our priorities.
